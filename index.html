Seoyun (Yuni) Jeong

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at UMass Amherst, where I am fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. Daniel Sheldon and Prof. Subhransu Maji. I'm broadly interested in AI for science, particularly computer vision and multimodal models applied to ecological problems such as species identification, and statistical inference methods for modern AI systems. I hope to eventually collaborate with researchers across fields to apply AI-based tools to areas like agriculture, healthcare, and sustainability.

Before starting the PhD, I completed BS degrees in Mathematics and Computer Science (2020-2023) and an accelerated MS (2024), both at UMass Amherst.
